## Abstract

Porous carbon electrodes are attractive for supercapacitors because of their high specific surface area.

We prepared a porous carbon electrode from resorcinol and formaldehyde and measured its specific capacitance in 1 M H2SO4.

## Results

The porous carbon electrode reached a specific capacitance of 210 F/g at 1 A/g.

After 5000 cycles, the specific capacitance retained 92 % of its initial value (Figure 1).

## Discussion

These results suggest that the porous carbon electrode is stable under cycling, although the specific capacitance at 5 A/g was not reported [1].
